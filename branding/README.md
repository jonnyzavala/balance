# Balance branding

Preserve versioned master artwork and exported app icons here. Elemental order clockwise: Air (top, gold), Water (right, blue), Earth (bottom, green), Fire (left, coral). Keep each color localized to its quadrant; blended central lotus and opposing koi may use the full palette. Use a continuous angular spectrum along circular linework, with no repeated color regions. Palette: Air #DFC68C, Water #87B6D1, Earth #91B99B, Fire #D9937A, Spirit #B9A0D0. No personal data in this repository.
