# White Lotus achievement ideas

Draft catalog; not implemented unlock rules. No rewards for trauma disclosure, suffering, forgiveness or deliberately created setbacks.

## Berserk-inspired collection
- A Place to Rest — make intentional space for rest and comfort.
- Companions on the Road — share a meaningful ordinary experience with someone you care about.
- The Hill of Swords — optionally reflect privately on something you are ready to set down.
- The Road Ahead — take a manageable step toward a chosen value.
- Campfire Company — share a quiet moment together.
