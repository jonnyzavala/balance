# Balance — living product specification
## Purpose
Private personal companion for intentional living, wellbeing, relationship initiative, shared responsibilities and individual interests. Support recovery and sustained balance, not perfection, guilt, or relationship scoring. Real-world actions matter more than app engagement. No negative streaks or decay. Never quantify another person's happiness, affection, forgiveness or trauma.

## Identity and interface
Name Balance; repository balance. Gradient spectrum, cozy layered responsive interface, desktop and iPhone. Air north gold, Water east blue, Earth south jade, Fire west coral, clockwise. Outer artwork uses a single angular spectrum with smoothly interpolated hues peaking at cardinal points; center can blend freely. White lotus and opposing spirit koi, gradient linework master, simplified icon derived from it. Archive all branding revisions; supply favicon, Apple touch PNG, manifest icons. Preserve intuitive navigation, do not turn every feature into a separate tab. Day/night atmosphere, gentle sounds and motion controls including reduced motion. Version button with latest release open and bullet-point changelogs. Use Nauvis-style sync indicator: flashing yellow/green while syncing; solid green only after confirmed server sync; red for offline, errors, conflicts and local-only. Never imply a cloud sync when not configured.

## Daily features
- Today: immediate intentions, quick log, upcoming opportunities and things to look forward to.
- Solar Powered: city/date-based sunrise/sunset, preferred home-before-dark buffer, remaining daylight with a slow ~5-second warm breathing glow, time-of-day gradients. Rain/cozy override (The Jasmine Dragon), steam from coffee, warm orange year-round string lights, optional rain ambience. Manual override.
- Foundation: flexible full/reduced/recovery modes, daylight, morning activation, movement, personal time, reading/art/gaming, household and study routines; protect sleep and leisure without treating hobbies as rewards that must be earned. Intentional occasional late gaming rather than nightly; consider sleep needs and next-day wake time. Track joining partner in bed separately from sleep onset, wake time and rising, manual estimates and missing data.
- Our Home: own responsibilities from noticing to closing loop; recurring obligations vs flexible reminders; no partner oversight required.
- Little Rituals: configurable intervals, days since/days until suggested, gentle opportunities not red overdue states for affectionate gestures. Flowers weekly/biweekly as adjustable examples, not universal rules. Custom rituals, pause, snooze, log and undo.
- Adventures: Don't Overthink It (short, low-cost, low-prep outing suggestions), filters for budget/time/energy/daylight/weather; easy spontaneous logging. Adventure Inbox for links, text/screenshots, optional AI extraction with caption fallback, verify logistics; never send private journal content to AI automatically. Ideas → ready → planned → completed; skip stages for spontaneous activities. Things to Look Forward To, countdowns and calendar export, possible consented calendar integration. Distinguish intentional connection, parallel play and independent time, all valid.
- Activity log is canonical event stream; log planned or unplanned actions, edit/backdate/undo, optional notes/photos, custom categories. One action feeds relevant ritual, insights and rewards, no duplicate logging. History calendar and filters.
- Air Temple Island: evolving cozy pixel world, customizable avatars, themed decor, day/night and rain, animations, optional sound, screenshot/short GIF export. No hunger/affection meter or decay. Unlock from real activities.
- White Lotus: achievements and collectibles, permanent milestones, repeatable celebrations, secrets and seasonal content; recognize returning without rewarding deliberately caused setbacks. Optional private reflection achievements, never require disclosures. Inspirations: Avatar, Star Wars, Cult of the Lamb, Fifth Element, Godzilla, Dune, Lord of the Rings, Alien, Samurai Jack, Over the Garden Wall, Shape of Water, Returnal, SAROS, Last of Us, Outer Wilds, Berserk. Original art and sound; private interpretations remain outside repository.
- Harmonic Convergence: longitudinal sleep, mood, energy, anxiety, activation, daylight/outdoor time, hobby balance, routines and activities; quick self-report, missing data not zero, descriptive associations not causal diagnoses. Optional Apple Health bridge later; manual sleep entry supported.
- Sanctuary: private journal and clinician appointment preparation, selective exports; no automatic diagnosis or medication recommendations.

## Architecture and privacy
Blank onboarding with user-configured names/city, no real data in public source. Local-first, offline cache, robust sync/conflict handling, backups and undo. Separate Supabase project. Persistent sessions, secure pairing, named registered devices, current-device identification, revocation (including all other devices); server must reject revoked access. Owner and future partner roles: explicitly shared calendar/adventures/world only; private health, reflections, surprise gifts and personal metrics not accessible to partner even via API. Separate credentials/role grants, server enforcement (RLS or verified equivalent), no secrets in repo. Revocation cannot guarantee deletion of offline copies; communicate clearly. Optional local-only sensitive data; test with fictional data. No real clinical information until security verified.

## Pairing and responsive UI refinement (v0.6)
Owner devices support either a single-use 10-minute code or an optional persistent 256-bit owner pairing/recovery key, generated client-side, shown once for private password-manager storage, SHA-256 hash only in database. Key rotation/disable does not disconnect existing devices; revoking any device invalidates all outstanding invites and saved key to prevent reuse by revoked parties. Existing active devices may generate a new key. Never use owner pairing for future restricted partner access. Show explicit success in pairing dialog without requiring closure, distinguish registered from server-synced; disable request buttons during busy state. Desktop layout compact with smaller typography, cards and buttons; preserve touch-sized mobile controls and 16px mobile input text.

## Visual redesign phase
The v0.6 UI is functional scaffolding, not approved final design. Perform a cohesive visual redesign rather than accumulating small cosmetic patches: calm warm layered identity, intentional hierarchy, contextual atmosphere, expressive original pixel-world scenes, accessible and responsive navigation, coherent typography and original motion/audio with reduced-motion controls. Today is the central daily dashboard, with secondary features organized by use rather than a tab for every concept. Settings is a header gear alongside version and sync; keep it out of the primary navigation. Preserve current functionality, data compatibility and privacy boundaries through redesign. Desktop density and mobile touch usability must both be tested. No claim of final visual approval without user review.

## Warm tactile visual language (v0.7)
User favors the coziness and tactile visual feedback of Nintendo interfaces: warm elemental color, meaningful drop shadows, raised controls, layered panels, friendly rounded shapes, and contextual lighting rather than flat gray utility UI. Default warm light theme, optional cozy dark and automatic day/night. Per-device theme choice (not synced) in Settings. Contextual morning, daylight, sunset, rain and night palettes use existing manual daylight times and atmosphere override until live integration. Header sync on left; centered identity; uniform version and gear-only settings controls at right, accessible labels retained. Desktop Today two-column hero and mobile single-column flow. Preserve data/state behavior; future artwork and richer hierarchy remain open to refinement. This is an initial redesign pass, not final visual approval.
